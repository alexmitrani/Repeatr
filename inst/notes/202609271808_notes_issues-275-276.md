## Key decisions
- **Graygoo, not Greygoo.** The issue title says "Graygoo" and the body says "Greygoo". The FLS page for the show has a user comment that settles it: "Openers were Ottawa's Shotmaker and Quebec City's Graygoo."
- **Fixed in `Repeatr_1()`, not in `fls_data.csv`.** `fls_data.csv` is re-scraped from dischord.com, and the site's own "Played with" field still says only "Shot Maker". A hand edit there would be lost on the next scrape. This follows the existing per-gid overrides and band-name spelling corrections in the played_with section.
- **"Shot Maker" is renamed to "Shotmaker" everywhere, not just for this show.** The raw data also spells it "Shot Maker" for Montreal 9/23/95 (`montreal-qc-canada-92395`), while Ottawa 9/26/95 already says "Shotmaker". All three shows now use the same name. The band's summary row in `played_with_summary` for the 1995 Fall USA/Canada Tour now shows 3 shows.
- Bands for a show are sorted alphabetically, as they are everywhere else. The recap therefore reads "with Graygoo and Shotmaker".
- **One function, two variants.** `recap_tracklist_columns_note()` now takes `format = c("pdf", "online")`. The two sentences about the parenthesised figures only apply to the PDF, because only the PDF combines each value with its series-wide mean or total in one cell.
